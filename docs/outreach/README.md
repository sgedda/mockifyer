# Conference outreach

Speaker kit for showcasing **Mockifyer** at testing and engineering conferences — starting with [TestCon Europe](https://testcon.lt/) (Vilnius, 20–23 Oct 2026).

This is **not** a product launch plan. Programme committees reject vendor pitches. The kit is a practitioner talk: a test-data pattern you can steal on Monday, demonstrated with Mockifyer.

## Verdict (2 September 2026)

**Yes — TestCon is the right *kind* of conference.** Audience is ~71% testers/QA, 56% senior, 35+ countries, cinema venue (huge screens — live demo works). Published 2026 themes include **Test Data Engineering**, **TestOps**, **Mobile Testing**, and **Traceability**. That is Mockifyer’s home turf.

**A 45-minute slot on the 2026 programme is unlikely.** The [schedule is already live](https://testcon.lt/schedule/) (announced 10 Aug 2026). Late Bird tickets end **10 September 2026**. The CFP form is still up (rolling / replacement speakers through 19 Oct), so submit anyway as a **backup speaker**, and treat **2027** as the real TestCon target.

**The urgent CFP is EuroSTAR 2027** (Copenhagen, 11–14 May 2027). Submissions close **30 September 2026**. Same talk, different form.

## Do this week

1. Paste the talk from [cfp-talk.md](./cfp-talk.md) into **EuroSTAR 2027** — [call for speakers](https://conference.eurostarsoftwaretesting.com/call-for-submissions/) — deadline **30 Sep 2026**.
2. Submit the same talk to **TestCon 2026** as a late/backup proposal: [Airtable form](https://airtable.com/appwHoM853A0uvnaR/pag6bCDEHgNxguykQ/form) + email `speakers@testcon.lt` (draft in the CFP doc).
3. Submit to **DevDays Europe 2027** (Riga, 25–28 May 2027, same organiser as TestCon, developer-heavy audience): [Airtable form](https://airtable.com/appHxAEL0xN1OErFQ/pag3NDDzoyeWtaF4H/form).
4. Decide whether to **attend TestCon 2026** on a Late Bird ticket (ends 10 Sep). Attending this year is the strongest path to being invited next year.
